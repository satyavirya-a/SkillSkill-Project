Our Journey

  • 2023SkillSkill Founded

    SkillSkill was started in Indonesia by a small independent team focused on building modern, easy-to-use digital learning platforms.

  • 2024Launch of SkillVerse

    The first brand under the company umbrella, SkillVerse, was launched with curated courses and a smooth onboarding experience.

  • 2025VisualSkill Goes Live

    VisualSkill was introduced as a dedicated platform for visual learners, combining design thinking, UI practice, and front-end implementation.

  • 2026Brand & Team Expansion

    Both platforms continue to grow competitively while opening opportunities for collaborators, mentors, and future team members.
